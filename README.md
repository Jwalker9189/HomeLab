# My IT Homelab

## Introduction

I originally wanted to build a homelab because, honestly, I thought they looked cool. But beyond that, I wanted a way to gain hands-on experience with different technologies, especially with how competitive the IT job market is right now.

I also wanted somewhere I could experiment with self-hosted services like Plex, network-wide ad blocking, and other projects without having to mess with my gaming PC. Basically, I wanted my own environment where I could tinker, break things, troubleshoot, and learn along the way.

## Purpose

My decision to pursue a career in IT comes from my personal interest in technology. It's something I've always enjoyed messing with, and I like that technology is constantly evolving. I figured if I'm already spending my free time learning about and experimenting with technology, why not turn that interest into a career?

As I continue building my homelab, my focus is shifting toward Microsoft enterprise administration, including Windows Server, Active Directory, Microsoft 365, and other technologies commonly used in professional IT environments. Hopefully this helps me get the experience while looking for my first IT job.

## Future Plans

- **Windows Server:** Learn how to install, configure, and manage Windows Server environments.
- **Active Directory:** Practice managing users, groups, permissions, and Group Policy.
- **Microsoft 365:** Gain experience with Microsoft 365 administration, Entra ID, and Exchange Online.
- **PowerShell:** Learn to automate common administrative tasks.
- **Windows Networking:** Configure and troubleshoot DNS, DHCP, and domain connectivity.
- **Self-Hosting:** Eventually set up services like Plex and network-wide ad blocking.
- **IT Troubleshooting:** Build realistic scenarios to practice diagnosing and resolving common enterprise IT issues.
